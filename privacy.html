Privacy Policy.

H23 Works · Hyderabad, India

1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how H23 Works handles personal information submitted through this website, including information provided through the enquiry form, email links, WhatsApp links and other direct communications.

2. Information we may collect

When you contact H23 Works, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, company name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the service you are interested in, and the contents of your message. We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to understand and respond to an enquiry.

3. Why we use the information

  • To respond to enquiries and requests for information.
  • To understand the services you are asking about and prepare an appropriate response or proposal.
  • To communicate with you about a business enquiry you initiated.
  • To maintain reasonable records of business communications and protect our legitimate business interests where permitted by applicable law.

4. How the website enquiry form works

At present, the enquiry form is configured to prepare an email in the visitor's email application rather than silently storing the form submission in a website database. The information is therefore transmitted through the visitor's chosen email service when they send the message. If H23 later enables a server-side form, Google Apps Script, spreadsheet, CRM or another processing service, this policy and the form notice will be updated to describe that processing before or when it is introduced, as applicable.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers that support communications, hosting, website operation or other business functions, but only as appropriate for the relevant purpose and subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

6. Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to an enquiry, managing an ongoing business relationship, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving disputes, or meeting legal obligations. We will seek to avoid retaining personal information longer than necessary.

7. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law and the provisions in force at the relevant time, you may contact H23 Works regarding access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, withdrawal of consent where consent is the applicable basis, or other applicable rights. Requests can be sent to h23workss@gmail.com. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we also encourage visitors not to submit highly sensitive or unnecessary information through a general business enquiry form.

9. Cookies and analytics

The current H23 website is not intentionally configured with advertising trackers or a third-party analytics platform. If analytics, advertising, cookies or similar technologies are introduced later, H23 will review the applicable notice and consent requirements and update this policy as appropriate.

10. Children

This website is intended for business and general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal data through the enquiry form.

11. Grievances and privacy contact

For privacy questions, requests or complaints about personal information handled through this website, contact: h23workss@gmail.com. H23 Works will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, data practices, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Important note

This policy is a website implementation draft prepared for H23 Works and should be reviewed and approved by an Indian privacy/legal professional before being relied on as a definitive statement of legal compliance.
